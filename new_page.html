CLEAR ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

Find straightforward answers regarding Canadian shipping, warranty coverage, in-store repairs, and product selection.

Do you offer free shipping across Canada?

Yes! We offer free standard shipping on orders over $75 to all Canadian provinces with reliable carrier tracking. Orders are carefully packaged with shock-absorbent cushioning directly from our Calgary distribution centre.

How do in-store repairs work? Do I need an appointment?

No appointment is necessary. Simply bring your vacuum (canister, upright, or powerhead) into our Calgary showroom during store hours. Our master technicians will inspect the machine and provide a completely free diagnostic estimate before any work begins.

Do you stock genuine OEM replacement bags, filters, and hoses?

Yes. We stock Western Canada's largest inventory of genuine OEM filters, bags, belts, roller brushes, and motors for Miele, Dyson, SEBO, Duovac, Cana-Vac, Beam, Hoover, and Numatic. Using genuine parts preserves your manufacturer warranty.

Do you handle central vacuum installations for new or existing homes?

Yes, our certified installation team handles new construction rough-ins, existing home retrofits, inlet valve additions, power unit upgrades, and Hide-A-Hose retractable conversions throughout the Calgary region.

Which vacuum is best for shedding pets and high-pile carpets?

For homes with heavy shedding dogs and carpets, we recommend an electrobrush-equipped canister like the Miele Complete C3 Cat & Dog or SEBO Airbelt E3/D4 with motorized height adjustment. Their sealed HEPA systems ensure pet dander cannot escape into your breathable air.
