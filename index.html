FAQS

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about our products, services, repairs and store policies.

Do you offer shipping across Canada?

Yes, we ship vacuum cleaners, accessories and parts nationwide across Canada with reliable and secure delivery.

Do you provide vacuum repairs and servicing?

Yes! We have fully authorized repair technicians on site who service all makes and models, including Dyson, Miele, SEBO, Duovac, Hoover, and commercial floor equipment. Free estimates are provided.

Can I get replacement parts for my vacuum?

Absolutely. We stock one of Western Canada's largest inventories of genuine OEM filters, bags, belts, hoses, power heads, motors, and replacement components for residential and commercial vacuums.

Do you offer central vacuum installation?

Yes, our certified installation team handles new construction central vacuum rough-ins, retrofits in existing homes, piping upgrades, retractable hose (Hide-A-Hose) systems, and power unit replacements.

Which vacuum is best for pet hair?

Vacuums with electrobrush powerheads and certified sealed HEPA filtration (such as Miele Complete C3 Cat & Dog, SEBO Felix/E3, or Dyson Outsize with hair screw tools) provide the best agitation without tangling pet hair.
